Wave Refraction Simulation
波的折射模擬

保持頻率固定，改變水深及入射角，觀察波在深水與淺水的清晰分界面上， 波長、波陣面間距及折射角如何改變。
Keep the frequency fixed and vary the water depth and incident angle to see how the wavelength, wavefront spacing, and refraction angle change at the sharp boundary between deep and shallow water.

Controls 控制
在淺水近似下，波速為 v = √(gh)。
Under the shallow-water approximation, the wave speed is v = √(gh).
減少水深會令波長變短，並使波向法線方向偏折。
Reducing the depth makes the wavelength shorter and bends the wave toward the normal.
角度由界面的法線量度。你亦可直接拖動畫面中入射波線上的橙色控制點來改變角度。
The angle is measured from the normal to the interface. You can also drag the orange handle on the incident ray to change the angle directly.

Suggested teaching use 建議用法
1. 固定入射角，減少右側水深，比較兩區的波長。
Fix the incident angle and reduce the right-hand depth to compare the wavelengths in the two regions.
2. 固定兩側水深，改變入射角，觀察波陣面在邊界處如何轉向。
Fix both depths and vary the incident angle to watch the wavefront rotate at the boundary.
3. 模型保持簡單：只有一條深水與淺水之間的清晰邊界。
Keep the model simple: one sharp boundary between deep and shallow water.
